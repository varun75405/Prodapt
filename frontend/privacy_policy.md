# Privacy Policy — AI Teacher's Assistant

Last updated: August 2026

## Overview
The AI Teacher's Assistant is designed to help educators quickly generate high-quality lesson plans, quizzes, and learning materials. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure experience.

## Information We Collect
- Username (for session identification only, not stored persistently)
- Topic inputs and generated content (processed in real-time, not stored)
- No student personally identifiable information (PII) is collected

## How We Use Your Information
- To generate educational content in real-time
- No data is stored after your session ends
- No analytics or tracking

## Third-Party Services
- We use Groq (groq.com) as our AI inference provider
- Your input prompts are sent to Groq's API for processing
- Groq's privacy policy applies to data processed through their service
- No other third-party services are used

## Data Retention
- No data is persisted beyond your browser session
- No database is used
- Generated content exists only in your browser's session memory

## FERPA Compliance
- This tool is designed to be FERPA-friendly by design
- No student education records are collected, stored, or shared
- No PII is required or stored

## Security Measures
- API keys are stored server-side only
- All inputs are validated and length-restricted
- Rate limiting prevents abuse
- Prompt injection guards are in place

## Children's Privacy
- This tool is designed for educators, not students
- No information is collected from children

## Changes to This Policy
- We may update this policy as needed
- Changes will be reflected on this page

## Contact
- For questions about this privacy policy, contact the development team
