🔒 Privacy Policy & Compliance

FERPA-friendly by design. Zero PII storage.

Overview

The AI Teacher's Assistant is designed with strict privacy-first principles for educational environments.

Privacy Guarantees

  • No Student PII: No student names, IDs, or sensitive personal identifiable information are ever stored or processed.
  • Server-Side Key Protection: API keys remain isolated on the backend server.
  • Session-Only Persistence: Generated content is kept temporarily in memory for your session and never sold or persisted in a database.
  • FERPA Friendly: Complies with educational privacy requirements by design.
